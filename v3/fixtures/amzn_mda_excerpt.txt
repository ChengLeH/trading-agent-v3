AMAZON.COM, INC. — Management's Discussion and Analysis (excerpt, fixture for V3 Prototype A)

Net sales increased 13% to $167.7 billion in the second quarter of 2026, compared
with $148.9 billion in the second quarter of 2025. The increase was driven
primarily by growth in AWS and advertising revenue.

AWS segment sales increased 17% year-over-year to $30.9 billion. AWS operating
income was $10.2 billion, with operating margin expanding to 33% as efficiency
gains from custom silicon offset higher depreciation.

Management expects AWS revenue growth to reaccelerate in the second half of 2026
as enterprise migrations resume and AI workloads ramp. Capital expenditures for
2026 are expected to be approximately $105 billion, weighted toward AI
infrastructure including Trainium capacity and data center buildouts.

North America segment operating margin improved to 6.4% from 5.1% a year ago,
reflecting lower cost to serve and strong advertising growth. International
segment returned to operating profitability.

The company recognized a pre-tax gain of $53.4 billion from the revaluation of
its investment in Anthropic. On an after-tax basis the gain contributed
approximately $41.8 billion to net income. Management noted this gain is
non-recurring and excluded it from discussion of operating trends.

Risk factors updated this quarter include increased disclosure around AI
infrastructure concentration and export controls affecting accelerator supply.

Advertising revenue grew 22% year-over-year, outpacing overall retail growth.
Management attributed the strength to sponsored product improvements and
expanding off-Amazon demand.

Free cash flow for the trailing twelve months was $18.4 billion, down from
$23.1 billion, reflecting the step-up in AI capex. Management expects capex
intensity to moderate in 2027 as data center capacity comes online.
